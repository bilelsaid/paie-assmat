# Privacy policy — Paie Assmat

Last updated: 31 October 2026

**Summary:** Paie Assmat does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

## Data the app handles
To compute pay, you may enter your name and contact details as employer, a child's first name, last name and date of birth, contract terms (rates, hours, start date), leave days and payments.

## Where the data is stored
All of this data is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device: the app has no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracker, and it does not request the Internet permission.

## What you can share
- Backup file: when you export a backup, the app writes a JSON file where you choose. That file is not encrypted; protect it as you would any document containing personal data.
- Shared message: when you tap Share, the app passes the text you can edit to the app you select. The child's address and date of birth are never included; the first name is included only if you enable it in Settings.

## Children's data
The app stores a child's name and date of birth because the parent employer needs them for the contract. This data stays on your device and is under your control. Delete it at any time from Settings > Delete everything, or by uninstalling the app.

## Your rights
Because no data is sent to us, we hold none about you. You can access, correct, export and erase your data directly in the app.

Results are indicative and are not legal advice.
