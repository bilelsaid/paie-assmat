## Short description
Monthly pay calculator for a childminder (assistante maternelle) employed by a parent in France, with the figures to enter in Pajemploi.

## Full description
Paie Assmat helps a parent employer in France check the monthly pay of a registered childminder under a 52-week monthly contract.

- Mark paid leave days on a calendar; working days, paid-leave days and the amount to pay are computed for you.
- A "What to enter in Pajemploi" screen lists the values to copy into the official declaration.
- Track payments and the remaining balance month by month.
- Paid-leave entitlement with the two common acquisition methods.
- Export and import your data as a JSON backup file.

**Your data stays on your phone.** The app has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics, and it does not request the Internet permission. It works without any network connection.

**Important:** results are indicative and are not legal advice. Check them against your employment contract and collective agreement.

**Availability:** for Android devices with HMS/AppGallery outside mainland China. The app is not available for HarmonyOS NEXT devices.
